UNDERSTANDING CONGESTION

Why Network Congestion Matters

Understanding the root causes and impacts of network congestion in campus environments

What is Network Congestion

Network congestion occurs when the demand for bandwidth exceeds the available capacity, causing data packets to queue, resulting in increased latency, packet loss, and degraded application performance across the network.

Why It Happens in Campus Networks

Campus networks face unique challenges: thousands of simultaneous users during peak hours, bandwidth-intensive applications like video streaming and online gaming, peer-to-peer file sharing, and limited infrastructure budgets.

How ML Predicts Congestion

Machine learning models analyze historical and real-time network metrics like bandwidth utilization, round-trip delay, and packet loss patterns to identify early warning signs and predict congestion before it becomes critical.
